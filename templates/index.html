PasteVault

Share text and code with a simple link.

Fast, lightweight, and distraction-free pastebin. Paste snippets, config files, notes, or error logs and share them instantly without creating an account.

Create a Paste

How It Works

Share any snippet in three straightforward steps.

1

Write

Give your paste a title, enter code or text, and optionally pick an expiration time.

2

Generate

PasteVault saves the content to SQLite and generates a collision-free 6-character short link.

3

Share & Manage

Send the link to anyone. Viewers can copy content in one click, and you can delete it anytime.

Key Features

Everything you need for quick text sharing, nothing you don't.

Simple Sharing

Compact, readable URLs like /p/Ab12X that make sharing fast over chat or email.

Optional Expiration

Set pastes to expire in 10 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, or 7 days, or keep them indefinitely.

Lightweight Storage

Powered by SQLite and SQLAlchemy ORM for instant queries and zero server bloat.

No Account Required

No passwords, no verification emails, and no tracking. Jump right in and create a paste.
